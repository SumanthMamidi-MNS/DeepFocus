Workspace Guide & Shortcuts

🎵 Ambient Auditory Studio

Choose a preset ambient sound or adjust multiple audio sliders to create a custom mixed soundscape that masks distracting room noise.

⏱️ Set Focus Duration

Use the adjustments below the timer (`-1 Min`, `+1 Min`, `60 Min`) to set your target focus duration before launching a session.

▶️ Press Play to Begin

Click the central **Play** button (or tap spacebar) to start the timer countdown. The tab title in your browser will display remaining time.

🔒 Deep Work Lock

Press the **Zen Mode** button (or `KeyZ`) to enter a fullscreen minimal interface. It locks out visual distractions and displays a breathing pacing ring.

🧠 Log Distractions

If you catch your mind drifting, click the **Log Distraction** button. Grounding your drift manually helps you refocus and alerts Zenith to track patterns.

📝 Reflect & Track Analytics

At session end, use the **Reflection Journal** to note down feelings and check the **Performance Stats** tab for cycle statistics.

🎨 Styling & Equalizer

Customize your interface anytime using preset themes, the color palette editor, or configure frequency gains using the 5-band EQ sliders.
